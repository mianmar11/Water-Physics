Purpose
    Create a fishing rod mechanic where the fishing bobber could thrown and
    float on the water. 

Specification
    A fishing bobber can be thrown using right click and it will have physics 
    applied to it. When thrown, it will go vertically upwards, then drawn down 
    by the gravity. It then checks if it collides with any nearby water areas
    and determines its ability to float or not. If it is in the water area, 
    it will become Buoyant and float on top of the water. Once it reaches to 
    the top, it will use 'wave_y()' function from 'Node' class. 

Implementation
    The fishing bobber `Bobber()` does not use 'wave_y()' function from 'Node'
    class when it reaches to the surface, however; the velocity of the bobber 
    works by using Water Boundary Checks and decides if the bobber should fall 
    down as in free fall or float up as in buoyancy depending on whether it is 
    inside water or not.
